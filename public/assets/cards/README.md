# Linear Search playing cards

Original raster artwork generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-19. Both images are opaque 1024 × 1536 images; WebP encoding is used for delivery. The browser draws live numeric ranks and suit text over the blank front, so assets cannot disagree with gameplay values. No SVG artwork is used for the cards.

- `sage-back.webp`: back of every card, 286 KB.
- `ivory-front.webp`: blank face, 85 KB.

## Exact generation prompts

### Back

Create one production-ready raster texture for the BACK of a playing card in a cozy pastel educational adventure game. Portrait 2:3 aspect ratio. Full bleed rectangular design, no outer canvas margin, no perspective, no tabletop, no drop shadow, no letters or numbers, no watermark. Muted sage green and dusty lavender with warm cream thin double-line ornamental border inset 5 percent, perfectly symmetric small repeating diamond-and-leaf pattern, small elegant central cream medallion containing a simple stylized four-leaf clover. Crisp printed paper illustration with very subtle paper grain, high contrast design that remains lovely at 75px wide. It should look like a genuine thoughtfully illustrated deck, not a UI button. Fill the entire image with the card-back artwork; corners will be rounded in the app.

### Front

Create one production-ready raster texture for the FRONT of a playing card for a cozy pastel educational adventure game. Portrait 2:3 aspect ratio, flat orthographic straight-on printable surface. Full bleed warm ivory paper with refined sage green and muted gold thin double ornamental border inset 4 percent, delicate tiny leaf corner ornaments. Leave the inner 78 percent of the card completely blank ivory for real numbers and suits to be rendered by the app. No numbers, letters, text, pips, suit symbols, medallion, characters or watermarks. No perspective, no table, no drop shadow, no margin outside the card. Subtle natural paper grain, crisp tasteful vintage playing-card print, warm and inviting, not a plastic UI tile. Artwork fills the entire rectangle; app will round the corners.
