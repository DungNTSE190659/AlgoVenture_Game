# Approved 2D direction

Created using the built-in image_gen tool. Backgrounds are opaque; islands have transparent backgrounds. Preserve these illustrations when regenerating UI sprites.

## Winter background — landscape and portrait

Reference: atlas/islands/linked-list.png. Create a new full-bleed winter game background using only the reference's flat 2D cartoon style and palette. Almost empty pale snowy blue center for readable UI. Small snow-covered evergreen trees, rounded rocks and snow banks only at the extreme four corners and edges. Thin colored contours, simple organic shapes, cream snow, muted evergreen, restrained flat shading. No horizon crossing center, realistic materials, 3D lighting, crystals, island, data blocks, characters, text or UI. Landscape 16:9 and portrait 9:16; recompose corner clusters for each orientation rather than stretching.

Outputs: linked/backgrounds/winter-landscape.png and winter-portrait.png.

## Tree island

Reference: atlas/islands/linked-list.png. Create a near-square rounded rectangular spring forest island in the same hand-drawn 2D cartoon style. A grassy clearing, shallow earth rim, one stylized branching deciduous tree at the back, rounded foliage clumps, low side bushes, sparse rocks and cream flowers. Foreground mostly open lawn. Soft moss, sage, cream and warm brown palette. No tall floating cliff, realistic textures, crystals, linked-list blocks, text, UI or characters. Fully contained silhouette with small transparent margins.

Output: atlas/islands/trees.png.

## Forest backgrounds — landscape and portrait

Reference: atlas/islands/trees.png. Create a new full-bleed 2D forest game background matching its colored contours and simple organic shapes. Almost uniform light sage/cream-green center for readable boards, significantly lighter than the island. Small cropped leafy trees, bushes, rounded gray stones and sparse cream daisies at extreme corners and outermost edges. Central 80 percent almost empty, including top/bottom center for controls. Restrained flat ground patches near edges. No central tree, island, horizon, 3D render, realistic textures, characters, text or UI. Landscape 16:9 and portrait 9:16.

Outputs: tree/backgrounds/forest-landscape.png and forest-portrait.png.
