## Island

Use case: illustration-story. Create a transparent PNG island asset for the ARRAY topic in a children's learning game. Use the reference only for clean 2D hand-drawn cartoon style, organic colored contours, simple shapes and near-square island footprint. New subject: a small peaceful village with three small cream cottages with warm muted terracotta roofs clustered at the BACK of a grassy rounded rectangular island, two simple leafy trees, a narrow pale river winding down the RIGHT edge and a tiny wooden footbridge. A short row of four adjacent blank rounded square stepping tiles near the village suggests contiguous array cells, without numbers or letters. Foreground half has open light sage grass with sparse tiny cream daisies and a soft sand footpath. The island silhouette itself should be nearly square, with a shallow earth rim, generous vertical ground area and small even transparent margins. Intentionally simple 2D storybook game art, limited palette: warm cream, muted peach terracotta roofs, soft sage grass and pale river blue. Thin colored outlines, restrained flat shading, no texture clutter. No winter, no fir trees, no characters, no text, no UI, no coins or badges. No 3D or isometric render, no deep floating cliffs, no crystals, no realistic lighting, no glossy materials. Entire island fully visible, actual transparent background. Square composition.

## Backgrounds

Create a new full-bleed background for a children's educational game using ONLY the reference village island's simple 2D cartoon illustration style, colored contours and warm cream/sage/river blue/terracotta palette. Very quiet nearly uniform pale warm cream-green central 80 percent for readable game UI. Small cropped village cottage and leafy tree near upper LEFT corner only, sparse low bushes, rounded stones and tiny cream daisies at extreme corners. A narrow pale river is confined to the outer RIGHT edge, mostly visible at the lower right corner, and NEVER crosses the center. Decorations small and peripheral, not a landscape scene with a horizon. Flat restrained shading, sparse intentional shapes, lighter and much less saturated than reference island. No array tiles, no central buildings, no island, no floating cliffs, no characters, no 3D, realistic lighting, text, UI or watermark. Opaque background.

Landscape suffix: Wide LANDSCAPE 16:9 desktop background.

Portrait suffix: Tall PORTRAIT 9:16 mobile background with ample clear vertical interior.
